Frequently asked questions

How long do dip powder nails last?

With proper care, 3–4 weeks — longer than regular polish and comparable to gel.

Is UV light used?

No. Dip powder air-dries, so there's no UV lamp time at all.

How do you remove dip powder?

We soak them off gently and condition your nails — never pry or peel them.

Do you do pink & white dip?

Yes — pink & white sets from $60 and any single color from $50.
