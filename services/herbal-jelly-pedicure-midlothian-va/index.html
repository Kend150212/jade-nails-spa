Frequently asked questions

How long does the herbal jelly pedicure take?

Plan for 60–75 minutes — it's our most indulgent pedicure experience.

What makes it different from a classic pedicure?

The warm jelly holds heat longer than water and the dissolved herbs soften skin deeply, giving you smoother heels and longer-lasting softness.

Is it good for tired, achy feet?

Absolutely. The warm jelly and extended massage melt away the day — many clients say it's the most relaxing pedicure they've had.

Is it safe during pregnancy?

Yes, the extended massage and herbal soak make it a favorite for moms-to-be. Just let us know and we'll adjust pressure for comfort.
