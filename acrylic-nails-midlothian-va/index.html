Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do acrylics damage natural nails?

When applied and removed properly — which is how we do it — your natural nails stay healthy underneath. Damage almost always comes from picking or prying them off; let us remove them safely for $20.

How long do acrylic nails last?

A full set lasts 2–3 weeks before needing a fill. With regular fills every 2–3 weeks, you can keep the same set going for months.

What's the difference between a fill and a full set?

A fill refreshes your grown-out set: we file down the product near the cuticle and rebuild that area. It's faster and cheaper than a full set — from $40 & up.

Acrylic or Gel-X — how do I choose?

Choose acrylic for maximum length, extreme durability, and the lowest price. Choose Gel-X for a lighter, more natural feel with no odor and gentler removal. Your artist can advise at your appointment.

Can I switch from acrylic to builder gel or Gel-X later?

Absolutely. We safely soak off your acrylics ($20) and start fresh with whichever system suits your goals.
