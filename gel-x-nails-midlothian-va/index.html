Why Gel-X

Extensions without the acrylic.

Gel-X is a soft-gel extension system: full-cover tips are adhered with gel and cured — no monomer, no strong odors, no heavy drilling. The result feels light and natural, with a thin, elegant profile that grows out gracefully.

Feels like your nails

Soft gel is flexible and lightweight, so extensions move with you instead of feeling rigid.

No harsh filing

Application needs minimal prep of the natural nail, and removal is a simple soak-off — about 15–20 minutes.

Any length & shape

From short and natural to long drama: almond, coffin, stiletto, or square. Specialty shapes are $5.

Clean salon air

No acrylic monomer means no strong chemical smell — a calmer appointment for you and everyone around you.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long do Gel-X nails last?

Typically 2 to 3+ weeks with proper care. Because they're soft gel, they wear a little more naturally than acrylic — most guests rebook around week 3.

Do Gel-X nails damage natural nails?

They're among the gentlest extensions: minimal prep, no aggressive drilling, and soak-off removal. Keep cuticles oiled and never pry a grown-out set off yourself.

Can Gel-X be filled like acrylic?

No — Gel-X is designed as a fresh set each time. The old set soaks off gently (15–20 minutes) and a new set goes on, which keeps the structure balanced.

Gel-X or acrylic — how do I choose?

Choose Gel-X for a natural feel, no odor, and gentle removal. Choose acrylic if you want extreme length or the hardest-wearing enhancement. Your artist can advise at your appointment.

Can I get designs on Gel-X?

Yes — gel color, chrome, cat-eye, French, ombré, and hand-painted art all work beautifully. Art starts at $15.
